Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a collage?

Open the app, tap New Collage, select your photos from the library, choose a layout template, and customize to your liking. Tap the export button to save or share your collage.

Can I edit individual photos in a collage?

Yes. Tap any photo inside the collage frame to enter edit mode. You can adjust brightness, contrast, crop, rotate, and apply filters.

Where are my saved collages stored?

All collages are saved directly to your device's photo library. Layoutify does not upload or store your images on any server.

The app crashes or won't open. What should I do?

  • Restart the app and try again.
  • Make sure your iOS is up to date.
  • Delete and reinstall the app (your photos in the system library are unaffected).
  • If the issue persists, contact us below.

How do I request a refund for a purchase?

Purchases are processed by Apple. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
