This Privacy Policy describes how Layoutify ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our mobile application. Your privacy is important to us.

Layoutify is a collage maker and photo editing app that works entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, including:

The app requests access to your photo library solely to let you select images for collage creation and editing. Your photos are never uploaded to any server. All editing and processing is performed locally on your device.
